OVERVIEW

Developing agriculture with a long-term view.

Agriculture is the resource foundation of the region — where PAA builds businesses tied to land, crops and local supply chains.

We approach this sector by building and acquiring businesses that can connect with processing, materials and markets within the same ecosystem.

The goal is not to expand for the sake of a catalogue, but to form durable production capacity that can support PAA’s other sectors.
