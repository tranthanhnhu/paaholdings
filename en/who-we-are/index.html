ABOUT PAA

Building businesses capable of creating long-term value.

PAA Empire Holdings is a multi-sector investment group focused on building, investing in, and developing businesses with lasting value potential.

Rooted in Southeast Asia, PAA seeks opportunities that connect resources, production capacity, technology, and markets to form businesses with solid foundations.

Rather than chasing short-term growth, we focus on building value that can develop and endure over time.

Headquarters in Vientiane, Lao PDR.

OUR ROOTS

Rooted in Southeast Asia.
Oriented toward global opportunities.

Southeast Asia is a region of rich potential — with resources, people, and production capacity that continue to grow.

PAA was built on the belief that when these resources are connected through long-term thinking, operating capability, and market access, they can become the foundation for businesses of real value.
